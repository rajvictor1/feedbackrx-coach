Use cases

Feedback moments that matter in residency and fellowship.

Formative

Mid-rotation feedback

Replace vague mid-rotation comments with specific behaviors, learner reflections, and documented next steps.

Summative

CCC review prep

Give clinical competency committees consistent, concise summaries across faculty and rotations.

Difficult

Performance concerns

Structure hard conversations with evidence-informed frameworks so learners hear the message and know the path forward.

Coaching

Faculty development

Help attending physicians and preceptors notice patterns in their own feedback style and improve over time.

Frameworks

Evidence-informed structures your faculty already trust.

Ask-Tell-Ask

Quick formative conversations that start with learner reflection, share the key message, and close with understanding.

Situation-Behavior-Impact

Specific, non-judgmental feedback tied to observable behavior and its effect on patient care or team function.

ADAPT

Prepare the conversation, align expectations, deliver the message, plan next steps, and track follow-through.

PEARLS debriefing

Simulation and clinical debriefing with reaction, description, analysis, and summary phases.
