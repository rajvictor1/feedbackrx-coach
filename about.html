About

Built to make feedback a learning conversation.

FeedbackRx Coach was created for healthcare educators who believe feedback should be clear, useful, and human-centered.

"Clinical training programs already have brilliant clinicians and dedicated educators. What they often lack is a simple way to turn feedback moments into documented learning, faculty growth, and program insight."

Our mission is to make giving and receiving feedback in clinical education simple, useful, and a genuine learning conversation.

Why we built it

Feedback in clinical training is high stakes, but the process is often underbuilt.

Most programs rely on hallway conversations, ad-hoc forms, or memory. FeedbackRx Coach gives those conversations structure without adding friction.

Clarity before the conversation

Learners and faculty prepare intent, identify behaviors, and choose a framework so the feedback lands with purpose.

Documentation during the conversation

Key points, agreed actions, and follow-up dates are captured in the moment, not reconstructed hours later.

Growth after the conversation

Private coaching notes and program summaries turn isolated feedback moments into continuous faculty and learner development.

Values

What guides us.

Evidence-informed, not opinion-driven

We build around feedback and debriefing frameworks that clinical educators already trust and research supports.

Learner-centered by default

Every feature is designed to help the learner understand behavior, commit to action, and feel supported in the process.

Faculty as learners too

Great clinical educators are made, not born. We give faculty private coaching insights so they can keep improving.

Privacy and trust first

Conversations about clinical performance are sensitive. Security, consent, and program-owned data are core to the design.
