Scenarios

Common feedback challenges we are built to solve.

Mid-rotation feedback

Residents stopped guessing what their preceptors meant.

A family medicine residency used SBI feedback mid-rotation. Residents received specific behaviors to adjust before final evaluations, and preceptors spent less time writing vague comments.

"Instead of 'read more,' I now know I need to practice presenting cases in one minute and checking vital signs before I speak." Illustrative learner quote based on design goals.
Clearer action items Less remediation confusion
Faculty coaching

Faculty started improving their own feedback conversations.

A medical school faculty development office gave educators private coaching records. Faculty reviewed their own conversations, set growth goals, and shared summaries for promotion files.

"For the first time I could hear exactly where my advice turned vague and try a different approach the next week." Illustrative faculty quote based on design goals.
Teaching growth tracked Promotion docs ready
Competency committee

CCC meetings got faster and more grounded.

A residency competency committee replaced scattered email notes with FeedbackRx Coach summaries. Each resident's progress was visible as behaviors, actions, and follow-ups, not impressions.

"We went from 'I think this resident is doing better' to 'here are three documented behaviors and two completed actions.'" Illustrative program director quote based on design goals.
Documented decisions Shorter meetings
Remediation support

Struggling learners got a path forward instead of a warning.

An NP program used private growth plans for learners falling behind. Each plan named specific behaviors, practice opportunities, and check-in dates so remediation felt supportive.

"I knew exactly what to practice and when we would meet again. It felt like coaching, not punishment." Illustrative learner quote based on design goals.
Specific growth plans Better learner morale
