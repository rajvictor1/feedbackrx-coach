Roadmap

Our security and compliance timeline.

01

Pilot foundation

Access controls, encrypted storage, consent capture, and basic audit logging for early partner programs.

02

HIPAA workflow review

Policies, BAA templates, and technical controls reviewed with healthcare legal and compliance advisors.

03

SOC 2 readiness

Formal controls, evidence collection, vendor reviews, and penetration testing before external audit.

04

Enterprise readiness

SSO, SCIM provisioning, advanced audit exports, and custom data retention for large training systems.
